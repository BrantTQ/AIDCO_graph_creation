\subsection{Step 8: Freeze and Report the Level-wise Selections}
\label{sec:step8}

Under the corrected workflow, the substantive representation-and-cutoff choice is already complete in Step~7. Step~8 therefore acts only as a freeze-and-report layer. Its purpose is to copy the selected models into a stable downstream record, document that the graph family is indexed by aggregation level, and make that frozen selection explicit for later atlas construction and export.

The direct input is \texttt{07\_levelwise\_selected\_models.csv}. Unlike earlier versions of the workflow, Step~8 does not run a second composite selector. The full candidate surface remains in Step~7, and Step~8 simply records the winning row for each level.
